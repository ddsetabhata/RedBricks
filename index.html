About Us

Building the Future,
Brick by Brick

DDS Eta Bhata, under the banner of Solanki Enterprises, has been a cornerstone of the construction materials industry. Our factory operates with state-of-the-art kilns producing high-quality red bricks that meet the rigorous demands of modern construction.

Beyond bricks, we supply premium coal, river sand, and offer expert woodworks — making us your one-stop solution for all building material needs.
